1) Noisy or Irrilevant retrieved chunks can mislead the model?

Yes, especially when they contain entities of th same type as correct answer. 

6cMFY1k9RzLR -> Ha confuso due entità molto simili tra di loro, persone, parenti, che hanno fatto entrambe un ruolo in "who played the mom on lost in space" ma June Lockarth ha fatto un ruolo da protagonista, scritto esplicitamente nella prima sentence.
	
This example shows that even when the relevant chunk is retrieved and placed first, additional distracting chunks can still mislead the generator. The irrelevant chunk mentions Anne Lockhart, who is an actress and a person closely related to June Lockhart. Since both entities have the same surname and belong to the same semantic type, the model selected the wrong entity. Therefore, the issue is not retrieval absence, but context noise and entity confusion during generation.
